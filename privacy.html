Plant Stager · Privacy

Privacy, plainly

What stays on your phone

Your spots, room photos, light readings, staged pictures, plan list and care log are saved only on your phone. Plant Stager has no accounts and no server copy of this data.

A backup file is made only when you make one, and it goes where you choose to save or share it.

Plant identification

When you tap Identify and choose or take a photo, that one photo is sent to our server, and our server passes it to Pl@ntNet, a plant identification service that works for us as a service provider. The photo is used only to name the plant. Our server does not keep it after it answers. We do not send your name, your spots or your network address to Pl@ntNet.

To limit abuse, our server keeps a small rate-limit file: the time of each identify request and a salted one-way hash of the network address (not the address itself). Nothing else is stored, and none of it is linked to your photo or your data. Every entry is deleted within 25 hours.

Prices

Price checks ask our server for public seller prices. The request carries only the plant id. Like any web service, our server sees ordinary connection details when it answers. We do not use them to identify you.

Camera and light

The camera is used for the light meter, room photos, the AR view and Identify. Light is measured on the phone, and frames from the light meter, room photos and the AR view are never uploaded. The one exception is Identify, described above.

No tracking, no sale of data

Plant Stager has no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers. We do not sell your data.

Deleting your data

Delete a spot to remove its photos and readings. To remove everything, open Profile and tap Delete everything, or delete the app.
