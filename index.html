Getting started

Tap Measure a new spot, take a light reading where the plant will stand, and Plant Stager shows the plants that suit that light. Open a plant to stand it in your room with AR, or stage it on a photo of the spot.

The light meter

A phone camera is not a light meter, so readings are a rough band. Confirm with the shadow test in the app, or type a reading from a real meter.

AR staging

AR needs an iPhone with a rear camera and a well-lit floor. Move the phone slowly until the floor is found, then tap to place the plant.

Your data

Everything stays on your phone. Make a backup from Profile before you change phones.
